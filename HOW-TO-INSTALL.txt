EASEL STUDIO - HOW TO INSTALL
=============================

Easel Studio is a web app that installs like a normal app (a "PWA").
Once installed it opens in its own window, has its own icon, and works offline.

An app like this has to be opened from a web address (https) once before it
can be installed. So step 1 is to put this folder online. It's free.


STEP 1 - PUT THE FOLDER ONLINE (pick one)
-----------------------------------------

Option A: Netlify (easiest, about 2 minutes)
  1. Unzip EaselStudio.zip on your laptop.
  2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up for free.
  3. Drag the whole "EaselStudio" folder onto the page.
  4. Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app
     (you can rename it in Site settings).

Option B: GitHub Pages
  1. Create a free account at https://github.com and make a new public repository.
  2. Upload everything inside the EaselStudio folder (index.html, sw.js,
     manifest.webmanifest, the icons folder).
  3. In the repository, go to Settings > Pages, choose the "main" branch, and save.
  4. Your link will be https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/


STEP 2 - INSTALL IT
-------------------

Android phone (Chrome):
  Open your link > tap the three-dot menu > "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
  The Easel icon appears in your app drawer.

Windows laptop (Chrome or Edge):
  Open your link > click the install icon at the right end of the address bar
  (or menu > "Install Easel Studio").
  It appears in the Start menu and can be pinned to the taskbar.

You can also tap "Install app" in the app itself, under Actions (wrench icon) > Share,
when your browser offers it.


WANT A REAL APK FILE FOR ANDROID?
---------------------------------
After Step 1, go to https://www.pwabuilder.com, paste your link, and choose
Package for Stores > Android. It builds an APK you can install on your phone,
or an AAB to upload to the Google Play Store.


UPDATING THE APP LATER
----------------------
1. Replace index.html with the new version.
2. Open sw.js and change  VERSION = 'easel-v2'  to 'easel-v3' (and so on).
3. Upload again. Installed copies update the next time they're opened online.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Your painting saves automatically on each device. Phone and laptop
  don't share paintings.
- Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes the saved painting,
  so export anything you want to keep.
- Exports: "Save to device" downloads the image; "Share" (on phones) sends it
  to your gallery, WhatsApp and other apps.
